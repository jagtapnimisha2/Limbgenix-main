Foot Care & Diabetic Solutions

Diabetes can severely damage blood vessels, leading to poor circulation and tissue death. This damage often affects the legs and feet, posing a significant risk for people with diabetes. Nerve damage can further complicate the issue by making injuries less noticeable, which allows minor wounds to progress into serious infections. These infections can escalate to deep tissue injuries, requiring surgery, and in extreme cases, amputation may be necessary. Therefore, good foot care is crucial to prevent such severe complications.

At Limbgenix, we offer advanced orthotic solutions for diabetic foot care. Our orthoses are crafted using state-of-the-art fabrication techniques and machinery. The materials we use are multi-layered with different durometer ratings and are FDA approved, ensuring high quality and durability. These materials are sourced directly from the U.S., guaranteeing the best possible protection and support for patients with diabetes.

Types of Foot Care & Diabetic Solutions

Custom Diabetic Foot Orthotics

Tailored orthotics provide support, reduce pressure points, and prevent ulcers, enhancing foot health.

Therapeutic Footwear

Specialized footwear accommodates deformities, provides cushioning, and protects diabetic feet, improving comfort.

Wound Care Management

Advanced treatments address foot ulcers, promote healing, and prevent infections in diabetic patients.

Regular Foot Health Assessments

Routine examinations detect early complications, ensuring timely interventions for optimal foot health.
